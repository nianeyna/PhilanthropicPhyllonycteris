## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-15

First release.

### Added

- Right-click anywhere on the chart to place a route marker; each further right-click adds another to the end of the route, and a line runs from the ship through them in order.
- Hold the right button on a marker and drag to move it; right-click a marker to remove it; double right-click to clear the route.
- A bat with a light around it hovers near the ship at zee, on the side of the next marker, and hides in port, while the chart is open, and once the marker is nearly reached. Arriving removes the marker.
- Routes are remembered between sessions, keyed by the layout of the zee, so every save of the same zee shares one route and an heir who inherits the chart inherits it. A newly generated zee starts unmarked.
- Settings for the bat's distance from the ship, the arrival distance, the colour of its light, and turning the bat off while keeping the chart markers.
